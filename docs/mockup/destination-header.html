Recommendation: B now, D once the trip is committed and the loop runs. The bar feels wrong today because it tries to be a summary and a search bar at once. Give the bar one job, saying where you are going, and let the sheet keep the totals it already shows; that is what makes the slot feel level with the search bar it replaces. When the committed trip exists, the second line comes back with something the totals cannot give: the next stop. A is the same idea with the totals kept visible, at the price of chips over the map. C is the cheapest way to keep one number in the bar without a second line. E is the honest Maps clone; taller than the search bar, so the swap animation stops being a fade in place.
