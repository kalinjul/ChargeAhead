Recommendation: A. It removes the double title by making the bar say what the page is about, it keeps the bottom row identical to the trip sheet so nothing new has to be learned, and it puts the two rare actions where Material puts rare actions: refresh in the bar, the destructive one behind the overflow. Every piece is a stock component the app already uses (AppTopBar with subtitle, its actions slot, a DropdownMenu). B if you want the totals to have more presence than a subtitle can give. C and D are prettier at a glance and worse to use: C hides three actions behind icons, D makes "Navigieren beenden" a list row that scrolls out of reach.
