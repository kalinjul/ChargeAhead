Trip list styles

Three ways to list the charging stops in the trip sheet, same data everywhere: Hamburg → München, three stops. The flag button is gone in all three; navigating to one stop happens from its detail sheet.

A · Itinerary rail

A line from start to destination, one dot per stop in the operator colour with the stop number. Rows are plain text, no borders. Section selection highlights a span of the rail (stop 2 shown selected).

Recommendation: A. The rail says "this is a sequence along a road" without a single card border, fits five rows in the peek, and section selection becomes a highlighted span instead of outlined boxes. B is the fallback if the rail feels too much like a transit app. C only pays off once the list has more stops than fit anyway.
